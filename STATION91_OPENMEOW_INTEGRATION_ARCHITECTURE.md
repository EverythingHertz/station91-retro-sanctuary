## Pillar 2: Strategic Solution Architecture & MeowCP Integration

### 1. Decoupled Media Assembly Invariant
Never prompt a single text-to-video model to simultaneously maintain character identity *and* simulate complex destruction.
* **Layer 0 (Static/Looping Interior Plate):** Locked at 100% pixel stability (e.g., `iter15_coder_loft_art_synth_aquarium.jpg` with polygon-masked rain in `render_iter15_video_loop.py`).
* **Layer 1 (Physics Plate):** Pure environmental chaos in Shorts, or 4:3 CRT shader gameplay in Long-Form VODs.
* **Layer 2 (Character & HUD Overlay):** Transparent PNG/WebM cutouts of Alf + Chromium WebSocket HUD (`station91_live_preview.html`).

## Pillar 5: Continuous, Data-Driven Iteration & Telemetry

### Weekly Algorithmic Calibration Loop (Every Sunday)
Track three primary telemetry signals in YouTube Studio Analytics and adapt the next week's batch immediately:

1. **Hook Hold Rate (0–3s Viewed vs. Swiped Away):**
   * *Target:* $\ge 80\%$.
   * *Action if $< 75\%$:* Increase visual contrast in Frame 1 (brighter neon magenta/cyan saturation) and move the tape-scratch audio interrupt earlier from `2.7s` to `2.2s`.
2. **Average Percentage Viewed (APV) & Loop Multiplier:**
   * *Target:* $\ge 105\%$ on 15s Shorts.
   * *Action if $< 90\%$:* Trim dead air after the secondary escalation (`11.5s–13.5s`) and cut the final frame 200ms after Alf's last syllable so the loop restarts before the viewer registers the ending.
3. **The Fatigue Horizon Trigger:**
   * *Signal:* APV drops $>12$ percentage points across 5 consecutive episodes in the same setting.
   * *Action:* Trigger **Phase 2 Lore Evolution** (shift from *1980s Aerobics Studio* to *Alf Invades Classic Nintendo Cartridges* or *The VHS Glitch Arc*).
