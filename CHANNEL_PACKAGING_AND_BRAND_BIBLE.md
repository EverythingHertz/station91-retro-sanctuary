## 4. Thumbnail Architecture & Click-Through Strategy

Using the `higgsfield-youtube-thumbnail` guidelines, our thumbnails follow a strict **Information-Gap Formula**:

### Rule 1: High Contrast & Authentic CRT Glow
* Avoid generic oversaturated MrBeast yellow arrows.
* Use deep navy/charcoal backgrounds (`#0B0E14`) contrasted against vibrant phosphorus green, amber CRT glow, and warm neon magenta accents.
* Always apply a subtle CRT scanline and glass reflection pass over screen elements.

### Rule 2: The Two Thumbnail Archetypes
1. **Long-Form Nintendo & Chill VODs:**
   * *Visual:* Wide cozy shot of the CRT playing a recognizable 16-bit scene (e.g. Mario walking through Donut Plains or Link at the campfire), rain on the window, steaming mug.
   * *Text Overlay (Max 3 words, Retro Font):* `1991 RETRO CHILL` or `MIDNIGHT SNES` or `DEEP FOCUS`.
2. **Viral AI Shorts:**
   * *Visual:* High-stakes visual contradiction. Example: Chad screaming in terror while Alf casually adjusts the controls of a wrecking ball, or an NES console smoking as an 8-bit mushroom cloud blooms on the CRT.
   * *Text Overlay:* `HE DID IT AGAIN` or `NO NO NO` or `INCIDENT #07`.

## 5. Live Concierge Bot: "The Night Receptionist"

The YouTube Live Chat API bot runs continuously during the 24/7 stream, fostering a dedicated, loyal community.

### Bot Persona
* **Name:** `Station 91 Concierge` (or `Night Receptionist`)
* **Tone:** Calm, slightly eccentric 1990s night-shift radio operator. Welcoming, dryly witty, never hyperactive.

### Live Chat Commands
* `!vibe`: Displays current track name, BPM, and synth instruments used.
* `!scene`: Explains the current visual room (e.g., "You are currently tuned into the 1991 Late-Night Officewave cell. Rain density: 85%.").
* `!alf`: Drops a random deadpan quote or status update from Alf (e.g., "Alf is currently napping on the audio rack. Do not disturb.").
* `!spotify`: Drops the direct link to the official Station 91 playlist.
* `!unlock`: Drops an ephemeral code (e.g., `STATION-91-FOG`) that unlocks secret room themes inside the web app.
