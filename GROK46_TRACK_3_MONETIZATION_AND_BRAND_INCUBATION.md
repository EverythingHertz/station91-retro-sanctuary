# GROK46 Track 3: Monetization & Brand Incubation

## P&L and Media Revenue Integration

### 1. AdSense Yield
- Modeled at **$2.40 USD RPM** equivalent per 1,000 monetized views.
- Requires strict adherence to the 72-hour plate and audio rotation policy to prevent Content ID flags, reuse policies, or demonetization on 24/7 streams.

### 2. Compounding Top-of-Funnel
- Channels serve as a standalone revenue-generating media business.
- They function simultaneously as the **zero-CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) acquisition funnel** for the SaaS subscriptions.
- The Live Concierge Bot bridging YouTube viewers to the web app using ephemeral unlock codes (e.g., `FOG-7K2`) directly feeds active SaaS users.

### 3. Cross-Platform DSP Streaming
- Expanding sound beds to Spotify and Apple Music editorial playlists.
- Generates passive streaming royalties while directing listeners back to the core visual application via "Share Your Sanctuary" card portals.
